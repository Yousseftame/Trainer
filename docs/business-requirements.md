# Digital Fitness Training & Nutrition Platform

**Project Brief & Scope of Work**

**Prepared by:** NODEX5 – Information Technology & Artificial Intelligence

**Submitted to:** Personal Trainer / Brand (To Be Confirmed)

**Project Name:** Development of an Integrated Digital Platform for Fitness Training, Nutrition, and Subscriber Management

**Document Status:** Initial Proposal for Review and Approval

**Target Implementation Timeline:** 4–6 Weeks

**Initial Capacity:** Up to 100 Subscribers

---

## 1. Project Overview

NODEX5 proposes the development of a specialized and secure digital platform designed primarily for mobile use (Mobile-First). The platform will enable the trainer to manage subscribers, deliver personalized workout and nutrition programs, track physical performance and progress, and provide educational content through a centralized digital system.

The platform aims to transform existing training materials, including PDF files, nutrition programs, and instructional videos, into a comprehensive and user-friendly digital coaching experience.

Each subscriber will have a secure personal account through which they can access a dedicated dashboard containing their workout and nutrition programs, instructions, videos, progress logs, subscription details, and historical records.

The trainer and authorized administrative staff will have access to a centralized dashboard for managing subscriber accounts, assigning programs, managing content, monitoring performance, and generating reports.

The platform will be developed based on the existing design provided by the client, with the necessary technical adjustments to convert it into a functional website.

**Primary Objective:** To create a professional and scalable platform that delivers an excellent subscriber experience, protects the intellectual property of the training content, and minimizes infrastructure and operating costs wherever possible.

## 2. Website Structure and User Experience

The platform will consist of three main areas.

### A. Public Website and Landing Page

A public-facing website that reflects the trainer's professional identity, experience, and services.

**Proposed features:**

- A branded homepage.
- Trainer profile and professional background.
- Presentation of training services and subscription packages.
- Registration or subscription request form.
- Secure login for existing subscribers.
- Contact information and a direct WhatsApp contact button.
- Arabic and English language support.
- A mobile-first design with functional compatibility across tablets and desktop computers.

### B. Subscriber Dashboard

A secure, personalized dashboard available only to authorized subscribers.

Subscribers will be able to:

- Access their personal accounts and manage their profile information.
- View subscription details and expiration dates.
- Access their assigned workout programs.
- Access their personalized nutrition programs.
- View exercise instructions and instructional videos.
- Open authorized PDF files through a protected document viewer.
- Record completed workouts and nutrition adherence.
- Track body weight, body measurements, and athletic performance.
- Upload progress photos and review previously submitted photos.
- Review previous workout and nutrition programs.
- View progress records and charts.
- Contact the trainer directly through WhatsApp.

Content access permissions will be determined by the subscriber's package, assigned programs, and subscription status.

### C. Trainer and Administration Dashboard

A centralized management system that allows the trainer and authorized staff to manage the platform's operations.

**Proposed features:**

- Register new subscribers and approve their accounts.
- Create accounts manually and manage login credentials.
- Activate, renew, suspend, and terminate subscriptions.
- Manage different subscription packages.
- Create workout and nutrition programs and assign them to subscribers.
- Create reusable program templates.
- Customize programs for individual subscribers.
- Manage the PDF and video content library.
- Monitor subscriber progress and adherence.
- Maintain historical records and previous programs.
- View basic subscriber and operational analytics.
- Export subscriber data and reports.
- Manage administrative user access permissions.

The head trainer will retain full administrative privileges and will be able to grant assistants and other trainers specific permissions according to their responsibilities.

## 3. Workout Program Management

The platform will support two workout delivery formats: PDF documents and interactive workout programs.

The trainer will be able to create reusable workout templates and customize them for individual subscribers.

**System features include:**

- Daily and weekly workout schedules.
- Exercise names and descriptions.
- Sets, repetitions, and rest periods.
- Training instructions and notes.
- Links between exercises and instructional videos.
- Workout completion tracking.
- Recording subscriber performance results.
- Updating and modifying workout programs.
- Maintaining historical programs and their different versions.

Subscribers will be able to mark completed exercises and enter the required performance data.

The system will maintain a historical record that allows the trainer to monitor each subscriber's athletic progress over time.

## 4. Nutrition Program Management

The platform will support nutrition program management through PDF documents and interactive meal schedules.

The trainer will be able to create, modify, and customize nutrition plan templates for individual subscribers.

**Features include:**

- Daily and weekly meal schedules.
- Meal descriptions and related instructions.
- Food quantities and nutritional notes.
- Calories and macronutrients, where such information is provided by the trainer.
- Supplement instructions, where necessary.
- Nutrition program updates.
- Nutrition adherence tracking.
- Historical nutrition plans.
- Individual nutrition logs for each subscriber.

The platform will serve as a tool for delivering and tracking nutrition programs approved by the trainer. It will not independently generate therapeutic or medical dietary prescriptions.

## 5. Subscriber Progress Tracking and Historical Records

Each subscriber account will include a dedicated section for tracking physical and athletic progress.

**This section will include:**

- Body weight history.
- Body measurements.
- Physical progress photos.
- Athletic performance results.
- Workout adherence rates.
- Nutrition adherence rates.
- Previous workout programs.
- Previous nutrition plans.
- Progress charts and comparisons.

The trainer will be able to monitor each subscriber's results through the administrative dashboard.

Subscriber data will be stored securely and will only be accessible to authorized users according to their permissions.

## 6. Digital Content Library

The platform will include a centralized library hosted on cloud infrastructure for storing and organizing the trainer's content.

**Supported content types:**

- Workout program PDFs.
- Nutrition program PDFs.
- Educational documents.
- Exercise instructional videos.
- Reference training materials.
- Various program templates.

**Expected video library size at launch:** Approximately 50–200 GB.

Videos will be stored on dedicated cloud storage and streaming services, using Adaptive Streaming technology to adjust playback quality according to the user's internet connection and device capabilities.

Authorized trainers will be able to upload, organize, update, and associate content with subscribers.

Subscribers will only be able to access content assigned to them according to their account permissions.

## 7. Content Protection and Intellectual Property

Protecting the trainer's proprietary workout and nutrition content is a core project requirement.

The platform will incorporate several content protection measures, including:

- Secure login and user authentication.
- Private cloud storage for files.
- Access control for digital content.
- Temporary or signed access URLs where supported.
- In-platform PDF viewing through a protected document viewer.
- Hiding direct download and printing options from the standard user interface.
- Watermarks containing subscriber-identifying information.
- Session management.
- A maximum of two active devices per subscriber.
- Content access restrictions based on subscription status.
- Logging of basic activities related to login and content access.

### Limitations of Screenshot and Screen Recording Protection

It is important to clarify that websites cannot guarantee the complete prevention of screenshots or screen recordings across all browsers and devices. They also cannot prevent users from capturing content using an external camera.

Accordingly, NODEX5 will implement practical protection measures intended to reduce unauthorized copying and sharing, limit direct file access, and help identify the source of unlawfully distributed content.

These measures do not constitute an absolute guarantee against content copying or redistribution.

Advanced Digital Rights Management (DRM) solutions may be considered in the future, subject to technical feasibility, device and browser compatibility, and additional costs.

## 8. Subscription and Account Management

The platform will support multiple subscription packages.

Subscriber accounts may be created directly by the trainer, or users may register themselves, subject to administrative approval before account activation.

**Features include:**

- Manual approval of subscriber accounts.
- Account creation and credential management by the trainer.
- Secure password creation and reset procedures.
- Management of different subscription packages.
- Manual subscription activation.
- Subscription renewal and expiration date management.
- Account suspension and reactivation.
- Package-based content access permissions.
- A maximum of two active devices per subscriber.
- Historical account and subscription records.

Payments will be collected outside the platform during the first phase.

Online payment gateway integration and automated subscription billing are excluded from the initial scope.

## 9. Notifications and Communication

The platform will support email notifications for account and subscription-related activities.

**Planned functionality includes:**

- Registration and account approval emails.
- Password reset emails.
- Subscription activation notifications.
- Upcoming subscription expiration reminders.
- A direct WhatsApp contact button.

Automated WhatsApp notifications may be added in the future, subject to WhatsApp Business integration requirements, the necessary approvals, and associated service provider costs.

An internal messaging system between the trainer and subscribers is not included in the first phase.

## 10. Reports and Analytics

The administrative dashboard will provide a set of operational and training-related reports.

**Basic reporting features include:**

- Total registered subscribers.
- Number of active and expired subscriptions.
- Subscriber distribution across subscription packages.
- Physical and athletic progress summaries.
- Workout and nutrition adherence rates.
- Subscriber activity summaries.
- Content usage statistics, subject to available data.
- Subscriber data and report exports.

Advanced predictive analytics, AI-generated recommendations, and financial forecasting are excluded from the first phase.
