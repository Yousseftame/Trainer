**Digital Fitness Coaching and Nutrition Platform**

## 1. Project Overview

Developing a specialized and secure web platform, designed primarily with a Mobile-First approach, aimed at enabling the coach to manage subscribers, deliver customized training and nutrition programs, track physical performance progress, and provide educational content through a centralized digital system.

The platform aims to transform existing training materials—including PDF files, nutrition plans, and instructional videos—into a comprehensive and user-friendly digital coaching experience.

Each subscriber will receive a secure personal account through which they can access a dedicated dashboard containing their training and nutrition programs, instructions, videos, progress logs, subscription details, and historical data.

Conversely, the coach and authorized management team will have access to a centralized dashboard to manage subscriber accounts, customize programs, manage content, monitor performance, and generate reports.

The platform will be developed based on the existing design provided by the client, implementing the necessary technical adjustments to convert it into a fully functional website.

**Main Objective:** To create a professional, scalable platform that delivers an exceptional experience for subscribers and protects the intellectual property rights of the content, while minimizing infrastructure and operational costs as much as possible.

## 2. Website Structure and User Experience

The platform will consist of three main components:

### A. Introductory Website and Landing Page

An introductory website reflecting the coach’s professional identity, expertise, and offered services.

**Proposed Features:**

* A home page with a design that reflects the brand identity.
* A bio section detailing the coach’s background and professional experience.
* Presentation of coaching services and subscription packages.
* Registration or subscription request form.
* Secure login for existing subscribers.
* Contact information and a direct communication button via WhatsApp.
* Bilingual support (Arabic and English).
* A Mobile-First design, with full functional compatibility across tablets and desktop computers.

### B. Subscriber Dashboard

A personal and secure dashboard available exclusively to authorized subscribers.

**The subscriber will be able to:**

* Log in to their personal account and manage their profile information.
* View subscription details and expiration dates.
* View their customized training programs.
* View their personalized nutrition plans.
* Watch exercise instructions and demonstration videos.
* Open authorized PDF files through a protected document viewer.
* Log completed workouts and track dietary adherence.
* Track weight, body measurements, and athletic performance.
* Upload and review physical progress photos.
* Review previous training and nutrition programs.
* View progress logs and charts.
* Communicate with the coach via WhatsApp.

Content access permissions will be determined based on the package tier, the specific programs assigned to each subscriber, and their current subscription status.

### C. Coach and Admin Dashboard

A centralized management system enabling the coach and authorized staff to control platform operations.

**Proposed Features:**

* Register new subscribers and approve their accounts.
* Manually create accounts and manage login credentials.
* Activate, renew, suspend, and terminate subscriptions.
* Manage different subscription packages.
* Create training and nutrition programs and assign them to subscribers.
* Create reusable program templates.
* Customize programs individually for each subscriber.
* Manage the library of PDF files and videos.
* Track subscriber progress and adherence levels.
* Retain historical records and past programs.
* View core analytics related to subscribers and operations.
* Export subscriber data and reports.
* Manage access permissions for administrative users.

The head coach will retain full administrative privileges, with the ability to grant specific permissions to assistants and other coaches based on their assigned roles.

## 3. Fitness Training Program Management

The platform will support displaying training programs in two formats: PDF files and interactive workout programs.

The coach will be able to create reusable training templates and customize them for each subscriber.

**System features include:**

* Daily and weekly workout schedules.
* Exercise names and descriptions.
* Number of sets, repetitions (reps), and rest periods.
* Training instructions and notes.
* Linking exercises to demonstration videos.
* Logging workout completion.
* Saving subscriber performance results.
* Updating and modifying programs.
* Retaining previous programs and their various versions.

Subscribers will be able to log completed exercises and input required performance data.

Additionally, the system will maintain a historical log allowing the coach to track each subscriber's athletic progression over time.

## 4. Nutrition Program Management

The platform will allow the management of nutrition programs through both PDF files and interactive meal plans.

The coach will be able to prepare, modify, and customize dietary templates for subscribers.

**Features include:**

* Daily and weekly meal schedules.
* Meal descriptions and preparation instructions.
* Food quantities and nutritional notes.
* Calories and macronutrients, when provided by the coach.
* Dietary supplement instructions, when needed.
* Updating nutrition programs.
* Tracking meal adherence.
* Retaining previous dietary plans.
* A dedicated nutrition log for each subscriber.

The platform will serve as a medium to display and track coach-approved nutrition programs and will not independently issue therapeutic or medical dietary prescriptions.

## 5. Subscriber Progress Tracking and Historical Log

Each subscriber's account will include a dedicated section for tracking their physical and athletic development.

**This includes:**

* Weight log.
* Body measurements.
* Physical progress photos.
* Athletic performance results.
* Workout adherence rates.
* Nutrition adherence rates.
* Previous training programs.
* Previous dietary plans.
* Progress charts and comparisons.

The coach will be able to monitor each subscriber's results through the admin dashboard.

Subscriber data will be stored securely and will only be accessible to authorized users based on their permission levels.

## 6. Digital Content Library

The platform will include a centralized library hosted on cloud infrastructure to store and organize the coach’s proprietary content.

**Supported content types:**

* Training program PDF files.
* Nutrition program PDF files.
* Educational documents.
* Exercise demonstration videos.
* Reference training materials.
* Various program templates.

**Expected video library size at launch:** Approximately 50 GB to 200 GB.

Videos will be stored on private cloud storage and streaming services, utilizing Adaptive Streaming technology to adjust playback quality based on the user's internet speed and device capabilities.

Authorized coaches will be able to upload, organize, update, and link content to subscribers.

No subscriber will be able to access any content other than what is specifically assigned to them according to their account permissions.

## 7. Content and Intellectual Property Protection

Protecting the coach’s proprietary training and nutrition content is a core requirement of the project.

**The platform will incorporate a suite of protection measures, including:**

* A secure login and user authentication system.
* Storing files on private cloud storage spaces.
* Role-based access control for content.
* Using temporary or cryptographically signed access links wherever possible.
* Displaying PDF files inside the platform via a protected document viewer.
* Hiding direct download and print options from the standard user interface.
* Adding a dynamic watermark containing subscriber identification details.
* Controlling active login sessions.
* Allowing a maximum of two active devices per subscriber.
* Tying access permissions directly to subscription status.
* Logging core activities related to login and content access.

**Limitations of Screenshot and Screen Recording Protection**

It is important to clarify that web applications cannot guarantee complete prevention of screenshots or screen recording across all browsers and operating systems, nor is it possible to prevent users from recording the screen using an external camera.

## 8. Subscription and Account Management

The platform will support multiple subscription package tiers.

Subscriber accounts can be created directly by the coach, or via self-registration subject to administrative approval prior to account activation.

**Functions include:**

* Manual approval of subscriber accounts.
* Creation of login credentials by the coach.
* Secure password generation and resetting.
* Management of different subscription packages.
* Manual activation of subscriptions.
* Renewing subscriptions and setting expiration dates.
* Suspending and reactivating accounts.
* Defining access permissions according to the package tier.
* Enforcing a maximum limit of two active devices.
* Maintaining a historical log of account and subscription statuses.

Payments will be collected outside the platform during Phase 1.

The scope of Phase 1 does not include integration with electronic payment gateways or automated recurring billing.

## 9. Notifications and Communication

The platform will support sending email notifications regarding account and subscription activities.

**Planned functions:**

* Account registration and approval emails.
* Password reset emails.
* Subscription activation notifications.
* Subscription expiration reminder alerts.
* Direct communication button via WhatsApp.

Automated notifications via WhatsApp may be added in the future, subject to WhatsApp Business API integration requirements, necessary approvals, and third-party provider costs.

Phase 1 does not include an internal chat system between the coach and subscribers.

## 10. Reports and Analytics

The admin dashboard will provide a set of operational and coaching reports.

**Core reports include:**

* Total registered subscribers.
* Number of active and expired subscriptions.
* Distribution of subscribers across subscription packages.
* Physical and athletic progress summaries.
* Training and nutrition adherence rates.
* Subscriber activity summaries.
* Content usage statistics, based on available data.
* Exporting subscriber data and reports.
